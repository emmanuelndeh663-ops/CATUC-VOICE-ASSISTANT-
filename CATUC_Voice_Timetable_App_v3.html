CATUC Voice Timetable

Faculty of Science · First Semester 2026/2027

1. Student information

2. Upload your course outline

Upload a plain text (.txt) or CSV (.csv) course outline, or paste its contents below. The app will look for course codes such as CSC 2101, ICT 2102 or MAT 1101. If your outline is a PDF or Word file, copy its text into the box first.

Your timetable will appear here after you upload or paste your course outline.

3. Your personal timetable

4. Ask your timetable

Type a question, for example: “What class do I have on Tuesday?” or “Where is CSC 2101?” You can also press the microphone button if your browser supports speech recognition.

Your answers will be based on your matched courses.

Important: this first version matches course codes from your outline against the supplied timetable. The Excel timetable does not label each timetable column by student level, so course-code matching is used to avoid assigning every class to every student. Please verify any unclear or missing course code with your department.
